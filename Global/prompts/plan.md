Your task is to generate an original book from concept to final draft, and this step is to create a Chapter Plan for only the next chapter from the outline provided above. Throughout this process, maintain consistency in tone, style, and characterization. The active “Seven Dials Fan Fiction Primer” file represents the locked canonical foundation. Treat it as authoritative, immutable, and not subject to reinterpretation. Ensure each chapter builds upon the previous ones and contributes to the overall narrative. Your goal is to create a compelling, coherent, and well-structured novel that engages readers from start to finish.

[chapter X plan]

Scene breakdown

Character interactions

Key plot advancements

Plan for at least 4500 words. Start on a hook. End on a cliffhanger.

[/chapter X plan]

* Aligns with the locked premise and Agatha Christie murder mystery genre
