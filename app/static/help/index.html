What SJB Writer is

SJB Writer keeps your books in plain text files on your computer and shows them in your web browser. It works without the internet.

When you want AI help, you choose exactly what the AI sees: your rules, your notes, your chapters, and an instruction. The AI then writes at your cursor. You can use online models through OpenRouter, or models that run on your own Mac through Ollama. AI is optional, and you can turn it off completely.

Starting the app

  1. Open the Terminal in the SJB Writer folder.
  2. Type cd app and press Return.
  3. Type python3 app.py and press Return.
  4. Open http://127.0.0.1:5050 in your browser.

Keep the Terminal window open while you write. To stop the app, go back to the Terminal and press CtrlC.

The screen at a glance

  1. Top bar. On the left: the logo (click it to close the document), the version, and the sidebar button. On the right: the AI setup panel, Visual layout, light/dark mode, Help, and Settings.
  2. Sidebar. Your project and its two sections: Background (notes) and Writing (the book). See Projects and the sidebar.
  3. Editor. The title and the text of the open document. See Writing.
  4. AI dock. The instruction box, the model, and the Generate button. See Working with AI.
  5. AI setup panel. Rules (System), saved instructions (Prompts), and generation settings.
  6. Status bar. Where you are, the save state, words, dialogue, reading time, and the size and cost of the next AI request.

Your first steps

  1. Pick a project. Click the project name at the top of the sidebar, or use the project ⋯ menu to create one.
  2. Create a document. Click the page-with-a-plus button next to Writing and give it a title.
  3. Write. The app saves for you. ⌘S saves right away.
  4. Optional: add AI. Open Settings, paste an OpenRouter key or install Ollama, and pick a model. Then type an instruction and click Generate.

Keyboard shortcuts

KeysWhat it does
⌘SSave now
⌘EMarkdown preview on or off
⌘↵Generate with the AI
EscStop the AI, leave zen mode, or close the open window
⌘\Show or hide the sidebar
⌘⌥\Show or hide the AI setup panel
⌘⇧FZen mode on or off
Tab / ⇧TabIndent or outdent in the editor

On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl where this table shows ⌘.
